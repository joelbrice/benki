# KYC/AML Policy and Risk Assessment

## 1. KYC Tiering Policy
### Tier 0 (Entry)
- Phone number verification + minimum profile
- Low transaction and balance limits
- Restricted corridors and services

### Tier 1 (Standard)
- National ID validation
- Name/date-of-birth checks
- Increased limits and broader services

### Tier 2 (Enhanced)
- Selfie + liveness
- Proof of address/business (where required)
- Highest limits and full service eligibility

## 2. AML/CFT Controls
- Sanctions and PEP screening on onboarding and periodic refresh.
- Name-matching with risk-based fuzzy thresholds.
- Transaction monitoring scenarios:
  - Structuring/smurfing
  - Velocity spikes
  - Dormant-to-high-activity transitions
  - High-risk corridor patterns
- Case management and STR/SAR workflow with full audit trail.

## 3. Risk Scoring Inputs
- Identity confidence and KYC completeness
- Device trust and SIM-swap indicators
- Behavioral anomalies
- Counterparty risk
- Geography/corridor risk

## 4. Monitoring and Governance
- Daily monitoring dashboards for compliance operations
- Escalation matrix for alerts and severe incidents
- Record retention aligned with local regulations
- Model and rule tuning governance with approval workflow
