# Operations Playbook

## 1. Support Operations
- Tiered support with clear escalation paths.
- In-app and assisted support channels.
- Standard macros for failed transfer, KYC rejection, and account lock.

## 2. Dispute and Complaint Handling
- Capture dispute type, transaction reference, timeline, and evidence.
- SLA-driven response and resolution windows by country rules.
- Root cause tagging to improve product and controls.

## 3. Settlement Operations
- Daily settlement job by provider/country.
- Reconciliation dashboard and exception queue ownership.
- Treasury and liquidity checks for high-demand corridors.

## 4. Partner Onboarding
- Technical due diligence checklist
- Compliance and legal checklist
- Certification for sandbox and production cutover
- Monitoring and operational readiness checklist

## 5. Business Continuity
- Defined RTO/RPO targets for critical systems.
- Backup restore validation cadence.
- Provider failover strategy and degraded mode playbook.

## 6. KPIs
- Transfer success rate by rail
- Average time to resolve disputes
- Exception queue aging
- Fraud prevention hit rate
- First-contact resolution in support
